## Scope and mode

Primary surface: `samples/dotnet/maf-magentic-civicworks/frontend/src/App.tsx`

Mode: Operate. This is the principal desktop officer workspace for a polished five-to-seven-minute demonstration.

## Audience, job, and task

A fictional NSW council officer reviews the initial investigation plan, follows synthetic evidence, understands why the sandstone-drain conflict changes the route, approves the revised plan with constraints, and inspects the bounded preliminary options brief.

The two consequential actions are approving the initial plan and approving the revised route. The product must always identify the human owner and present the output as decision support.

## Proof and constraints

The surface must show the precinct, current and previous plans, evidence state and lineage, changed route, remaining round/tool/time budget, operational specialist status, and unresolved matters. It must not expose chain-of-thought or imply autonomous engineering, heritage, expenditure, or works approval.

The principal walkthrough is keyboard-accessible and optimized for desktop, with a coherent stacked adaptation for smaller screens.

## Chosen direction

**Civic Routebook** draws from NSW civic wayfinding, transport diagrams, and public-works route notices. The investigation plan is a route the officer can see change. A continuous route crosses evidence stations, breaks at the sandstone conflict, and rejoins a revised route beside an officer checkpoint.

The memorable moment is the split between the superseded direct route and the revised heritage-survey route, with the causal evidence fixed at the junction.

## Unresolved decisions

The prototype uses deterministic synthetic content. Backend event mapping, exact run-state contracts, and final approved council branding remain implementation decisions for the full demo.
